# Shamit's LinkedIn: what's working (the feedback loop)

Updated every Sunday by founder desk A from Metricool data. Both desks read this before writing and must follow "This week's rules". Guardrails in the skill always win over anything here.

## How the loop works
1. Every post the desks book is logged in `founder/log.csv` (date, slot, format, topic type, hook type, first line, Metricool id).
2. Sunday: desk A pulls each logged post's results (LIPO12 impressions, LIPO13 reactions, LIPO10 comments, LIPO18 shares) and the follower change (LIEV01/LIEV08), matched by date and first line.
3. Score per post: impressions, and engagement = (reactions + 3×comments + 5×shares) ÷ impressions.
4. Compare by format (carousel / note / stat / photo / poll), slot (Mon/Tue/Thu/Sat), topic type and hook type. Medians, not averages.
5. Rewrite "This week's rules": KEEP (what beat the median), STOP (what was bottom twice), TRY (one experiment), each with the numbers behind it.

Rules for changing things:
- A bucket needs at least 3 posts before it can win or lose; until then it's "too early".
- Change at most 2 things a week, and only one experiment at a time.
- Slots may move by at most 2 hours, inside 07:30–18:00 UK. Never more than 4 posts a week, never 2 on one day.
- Never change the guardrails (facts, voice, minimal style, off-limits topics, no automated engagement).
- Metricool stats for personal profiles lag up to 3 days: only judge posts that are at least 3 days old.
