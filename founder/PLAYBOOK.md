## 4. Formats

| Format | How often | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Text post, first person** (900–1,400 characters) | 2–3 a week | Native text from a person is what travels on personal profiles in 2026; his own text post beat his video repost 3x. |
| **Text + build-log card** (his line, a number, or a real-colour photo with his hook) | every non-poll post | A free-licence Wikimedia Commons photo of the stadium, club or person in the story, credited in the first comment. Never agency, TV or club-owned images, never AI images of real people, never black-and-white or tinted. |
| **LinkedIn poll** | every other Saturday | 2–4 options, 7 days, a question fans and industry both care about. |
| **Personal photo posts** | when Shamit sends photos | The strongest format of all; only he can supply them (§7). |

**Every post except polls carries one plain image, no branding.** Off-white card, black text: his own line from the post (or a Thursday real-colour photo with his opening line under it), and "Shamit Manilal" small at the bottom. No logos, series names, slogans, "0 to Unicorn" or brand colours (owner's call, 5 Nov 2026).

**Writing rules**
- Line 1 under 200 characters, works alone before "…see more", **never a question**. Strong openers: a sharp claim, a dated fact, a number with a referent, a confession of a view ("I used to think fan apps were about scores. I was wrong.").
- One idea, one opinion, one number at most two. Paragraphs of 1–2 sentences with blank lines between.
- Ends with **one specific question** to the reader ("Club commercial people: would you put ticket pricing to a fan vote?"), never "Agree?" or "Thoughts?".
- 0–3 hashtags at the end: #SportsBusiness #FootballBusiness #FanEngagement #SportsTech #Startups.
- **No links in the post.** If a source link matters, it goes in the first comment (Metricool `firstCommentText`), with the photo credit.
- No "comment YES", "like if…", "repost to help", no tagging people to farm reach.
- Humanizer pass on every post: no AI vocabulary (significant, crucial, notably, comprehensive, insights, robust, leverage, foster, landscape, nuanced, streamline, elevate, unlock, game-changer, delve, tapestry, realm, navigate, pivotal, seamless), no "Here's the thing", "The result?", "Let that sink in", "Read that again", "Plot twist", no "No X. No Y. Just Z.", no one-word paragraphs, max one em dash per 100 words, at most one emoji.
- **UK times** in any copy ("kick-off 3pm UK"). UK spelling.
- Different from every ACTXION page post: never a story, deal or headline number the page covers within 7 days either side, never page wording.

### 👤 What only Shamit can do (LinkedIn bans automation of these on personal accounts)
This is where most of the growth comes from. About 20 minutes a day.

1. **Reply to every comment on his posts within the first hour** (posts go out at 08:00, 12:30 or 09:30 UK). Replies count as engagement and push the post to more people.
2. **Comment on 5 posts a day** from sports-business people, investors and founders bigger than him. A real opinion, 2–3 sentences, never "Great post". The ACTXION Monday pack already writes comment ideas he can use.
3. **Reshare the ACTXION page's posts with his own line** (Tue–Fri, 08:00–09:00 UK).
4. **Accept relevant connection requests and send 10 a day by hand** to club, sponsor, sports-tech and investor people who engage with his posts.
5. **Never use auto-like/auto-comment tools or browser bots.** LinkedIn restricts accounts for it.

## 9. Guardrails

- **His voice, first person, no inventions** (§3). ACTXION facts: "500K+ Google Play downloads", what actxion.com and the Play Store listing say, and anything in the facts bank. Nothing else.
- **Public facts:** one Tier 1 source (BBC, Sky, The Athletic, Reuters, FT, Guardian, SportBusiness, SportsPro, Sports Business Journal, Front Office Sports, Off The Pitch, Deloitte, UEFA, FIFA, official club or league sites) or two independent ones.
- **Off-limits (until Shamit says otherwise):** fundraising details, investor names, revenue, valuations, family, politics, religion, other companies' internal matters. Criticise decisions, not people. Nothing on deaths, injuries, abuse or players' families.
- **Held as draft, not published:** legal cases, investigations, disputes involving a named company, anything political.
- **Images:** Wikimedia Commons free licences (CC0, PD, CC BY, CC BY-SA), real colour, credited in the first comment; or photos Shamit sends.
- **Automation limits:** routines only publish Shamit's own posts through Metricool (LinkedIn's official partner). Never like, comment, connect, follow or message anyone; never touch the ACTXION page, its routines or skills.

## 10. Research behind this

- Personal profiles outperform company pages (Metricool LinkedIn Study 2026, 673,658 posts: ~63% more engagement for people; Ordinal and Refine Labs on reach). Sources listed in `linkedin/PLAYBOOK.md` §9.
- 2026 feed: dwell time, saves and real comments beat likes; engagement bait and pods were demoted in the "Authenticity Update"; external links cut reach (Ordinal link-penalty study ~26.5%), so links go in the first comment.
- Build-in-public and founder-journey posts are among the most followed formats for early-stage founders; first-hour replies from the author lift distribution.
- LinkedIn's User Agreement bans bots and automated access for likes, comments, invitations and messages, which is why §6 stays human.
