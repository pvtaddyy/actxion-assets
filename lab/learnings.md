## Current rules (from the owner and from research)
- Style = EP 01 v3 (engine/). Crisp SFX, no music under the voice, word-lit captions, context tag early.
- Biggest names and clubs travel furthest (Messi/Ronaldo, Haaland, Salah, Yamal, Big Six, Real/Barca).
- Physical metaphors beat charts: balls in tubes, goal grids, pillars, figures racing.
- Hook: curiosity / "one club he can't..." scored 78.7; aim 70+ on hookscore.
- Round 2 in the middle and the twist before the last 15 s.

- Comments: end on a one-word pick (e.g. "NEWCASTLE or BIG SIX"); open two-part questions got 0-1 comments.
