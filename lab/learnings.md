## Current rules
- Style = EP 01 v3 (engine/). Crisp SFX, no music under the voice, word-lit captions, context chip early.
- Biggest names travel furthest (Haaland, Kane, Yamal, Salah, Big Six, Real/Barca). Player-stat curiosity beats history on FB and YT.
- Curiosity hook ("one club he can't...", "nobody ran faster... you won't guess who") over number-first: same on IG, 4.6x on FB.
- Physical metaphors beat charts: balls in tubes, lanes, pillars.
- The hook works (IG 3 s view rate 67-81 %, desk 38-61 %). The problem is the second build-up beat: people leave at 16-27 s,
  before the twist. ONE build beat before the twist, never two of the same kind (two lanes, two ladders).
- End on a one-word pick (KANE or YAMAL). LAB pulls 2-4 IG comments; desk reels 1-2.
- Length: 54-60 s is fine, but shorter is not a lever by itself (see finding 3). Do not cut below 50 s to chase % watched.
- TikTok is flat for every format (desk 712, XI 544, LAB 673): do not judge content on TikTok views; read avg time only.

## What we learned
1. (15 Nov) Myth or Fact? Haaland (74 s, slot A) is the best LAB episode: IG 1,855 views (6.0x desk, 0.97x combined XI),
   26.8 s avg watch; FB 1,224 (4.6x desk); YT 977 (2.0x desk). A big name + curiosity hook works on every network but TikTok.
2. (15 Nov) Race Day (IG 1,627, FB 1,196) and Sounds Fake (IG 1,680, FB 260) are level on IG (5.2-5.4x desk) but Sounds Fake
   collapsed on FB (0.97x desk). Sounds Fake also used the number-first hook and the 01:30 IST slot, so the cause is not
   clear yet (THE MIC's 01:30 FB reel also got the lowest MIC FB views, 209).
3. (15 Nov) Length test FAILED: the 56 s cuts held 29-32 % of the video (16.4 s and 18.1 s) against 36 % (26.8 s) for the
   74 s EP 01, and views did not rise. Win needed IG avg watch over 50 % of length. People leave at the same point in the story
   (the second build beat) whatever the length.
4. (15 Nov) Exit beats: Race Day ~16 s = lane 2 (Frimpong, a second sprint like the first); Sounds Fake ~18 s = the "survive"
   beat (9 balls, climb to 14th) after the ladder; EP 01 ~27 s = Round one number run. Twists sat at 27-31 s.
5. (15 Nov) Comments improved with one-word picks: IG 3-4 per LAB episode (EP 01 had 1 at 20 h, now 2). TikTok still 0.
6. (15 Nov) Kane v Yamal at 12 h: IG 234 views but an 81 % 3 s view rate, the best hook hold so far. Judge on 22 Nov.
