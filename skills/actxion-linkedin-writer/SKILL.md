# ACTXION LinkedIn writer

Curated from sergebulaev/linkedin-skills (`linkedin-post-writer`, `linkedin-humanizer` V3 Sep 2026, `linkedin-comment-drafter`), marian-kamenistak/linkedin-post-writing-skill (anti-AI guide), kvsdileep/linkedin-writer and coreyhaines31/marketingskills (`copywriting`, `social`). Strategy lives in actxion-linkedin-growth.

## Voice
Smart, plain-spoken, a bit cheeky. A football person who understands money. Specific nouns (clubs, people, numbers, dates), short sentences, one clear opinion. Never corporate ("leverage", "synergy", "ecosystem", "excited to announce").

## Post anatomy (page post, 900–1,500 characters)
1. **Line 1 (under 200 characters, before "…see more"):** number-first or a sharp claim. Odd-precision numbers with a referent work best ("€1,161m. Real Madrid's revenue last year."). **Never open with a question.**
2. **Lines 2–4:** the fact pattern with the source named.
3. **The turn:** what fans think or feel about it, which the industry misses.
4. **The so-what:** 1–2 lines for clubs, sponsors or leagues.
5. **Close:** one specific question to industry people ("Club commercial folks: would you put ticket pricing to a fan vote?"). A closing question lifts comments; an opening one kills reach.
6. Optional **P.S.** line (small lift): a related stat or the app line ("P.S. 500K+ fans give their verdict on ACTXION every week.").
7. 0–3 hashtags at the end: pick from #SportsBusiness #FootballBusiness #FanEngagement #SportsTech #SportsMarketing.
Paragraphs of 1–2 sentences with blank lines. No links in the body (the app link is on the page); if a link is essential, put it in the first comment.

## Hook formulas that fit ACTXION (pick by goal)
- **Saves:** Odd-precision ledger ("€12.4bn. That's what the top 20 clubs made. Here's who paid."), Explain-it-simply ("How a club actually makes money, in 6 slides").
- **Comments:** Contrarian with receipts ("Clubs say fans are customers. Their pricing says otherwise."), False binary dissolved (both obvious answers fail), Answer-a-question-nobody-asked-fans.
- **Reposts:** Named tribute (a club or person doing fan engagement right), "end of an era" (an industry pivot, with dated facts).
- **Founder only:** Year-over-year pivot, build-log with real numbers, a lesson from 500K fans (only real numbers).
Use one contrast and at most one triple per post. Never stack devices.

## Formats
- **Carousel caption (Wed):** 600–900 characters: the hook, 2 lines on what's inside, "Swipe for the 3 things clubs should take from it.", closing question. `documentTitle` = the cover headline (under 60 characters).
- **Poll (Thu):** text 300–700 characters with context and the source; the question under 140 characters, 2–4 options of up to 30 characters each, mutually exclusive, one slightly provocative. Duration 7 days.
- **Founder post:** first person, 800–1,300 characters, one personal observation + one number + one opinion. Written so the founder edits 20% and owns it. Mark facts the founder must check with [check].
- **Comment draft (for the founder to post by hand):** 200–350 characters, no hashtags, no product plug, never "Great post". Templates: Missing piece ("The X argument misses one thing: …"), Answer the closing question with one concrete example, Data-first, Counter with concession, Sharper question.
- **Team reshare line:** one or two sentences, each person different (give 3 variants), personal angle ("We see this in the app every matchday: …").

## Humanizer pass (run on everything before it ships)
Score per paragraph; 3+ markers in one paragraph means rewrite that paragraph.
- **2026 AI-vocabulary markers:** significant, crucial, notably, comprehensive, insights, robust, leverage, foster, landscape, nuanced, streamline, elevate, unlock, game-changer, delve, tapestry, realm, navigate, pivotal, seamless.
- **Bridges to delete:** "The result?", "Plot twist:", "Here's the thing", "Here's what…", "Let that sink in", "Read that again", "What nobody tells you", "It's not X, it's Y" (more than once), "Let's dive in".
- **Staccato tells:** "Short. Punchy. Done.", "No X. No Y. Just Z.", one-word paragraphs, forced long/short alternation.
- **Performed sincerity:** "Let me be honest", "Confession:", "I'll be real". Replace with a flat, dated, uncomfortable fact.
- **Em dashes:** about one per 100 words at most; replace extras with a comma, colon or parentheses. Zero is fine too.
- **Over-correction guard:** don't make it deliberately worse; if a sentence is clean, leave it.
- **Facts check:** every number has a named source in the post or on the slide. No invented quotes.

## Lint before scheduling
- [ ] Line 1 works alone and is under 200 characters; not a question.
- [ ] One opinion, stated plainly.
- [ ] Ends with one specific industry question (or a poll).
- [ ] 0–3 hashtags, no links in the body, under 3,000 characters.
- [ ] Humanizer: no paragraph with 3+ markers.
- [ ] Every figure sourced; ACTXION claims limited to approved facts.
