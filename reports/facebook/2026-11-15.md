## 6. Plan check: the 19 Nov target will be missed by far more than half

The page needs 4,300 on Thursday and is at 4,064. Since the plan started on 5 Nov it has gained about 0 of the 229 needed, so the miss will be close to 100%. Image reach has not moved (median 16.5 then 15.5, target 150). Volume is not the problem: 18 image posts went out on time and reached about 15 people each. What should change:

1. **Images do not grow the page. Reels do.** Every reel reaches 10-80 times more people than an image. Recommend cutting image posts from 3 a day to 1 (keep 18:00, the best slot over two weeks) and using the freed effort for reels, unless E2 shows the still reel version of the card does better, in which case turn the cards into still reels.
2. **Use the full team reel cap with the two formats that win.** 3 team reels a day, made from Combined XI (20:00 IST) and stat surprises ("fastest player", "the club he can't score against"). Drop "who's to blame" debates.
3. **Africa has to be in the reels, not only the images.** The audience is African fans, but zero reels this week were African. E1 tests this directly. Put at least one African player in every Combined XI.
4. **Ask for the follow.** Reels reach 200-1,200 people but the page gains about 1 follower a day. Every reel should end on a clear ask: "Follow ACTXION for the daily debate." That is a format change, not more volume.
5. **Reset the targets with the owner.** 4,300 by 19 Nov and 5,000 by 5 Dec are not reachable on current numbers. A realistic short-term bar is followers growing every week, starting with net +20 by 22 Nov.
