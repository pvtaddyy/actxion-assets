## 6. Plan check: on course to miss 19 Nov by more than half

The first checkpoint (4,300 on 19 Nov) has not arrived, but the page has gained 0 followers since the plan started against the 234 it needs. Image post reach went from about 20 to 10. Adding more image posts will not fix this. What should change:

1. **Reels are the growth format, images are not.** Every new-style reel since 4 Nov passed 200 views; image posts reach 10. Fill the team reel cap (3 a day) every day with the formats that work (Combined XI, LAB stat myths, THE MIC), and test turning image cards into still reels (E2). If E2 wins, swap at least two of the three daily image posts to reels.
2. **Lead with Africa.** The best image topics this week were African. Next week has South Africa v Kenya (Mosimane v McCarthy) on 13 and 17 Nov and the CAF awards run-up. Give the narrated reel at least two African stories this week, and add an African player to every Combined XI where it fits.
3. **Write for the audience's clock.** Captions give kick-offs in "UK" time. Use WAT/EAT (or CAT) instead, and ask questions an African fan answers about their own team or player.
4. **Move the reel slot earlier if it keeps lagging.** The 16:00 IST reel (LAB) beat every 21:30 narrated reel; 21:30 IST is 17:00 WAT / 19:00 EAT, fine, but not proven best.
