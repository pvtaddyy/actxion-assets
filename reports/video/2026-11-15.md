## What worked
- **Fact-first hooks hold attention.** All 7 counted reels beat the news-reel watch baseline (5.7-11.4 s v 4-6 s), and 5 of 7 beat ~200 views. Across two weeks, fact-first openers have a median of 374 IG views and ~8 s watch; question or "you make the call" openers did 61 and 246 with ~6 s. Kept as the baseline.
- **Carrick sacked (Fans say...)** had the best watch time (11.4 s) and the only shares and saves of the week's new reels; testing a pundit's claim with numbers works.
- **Fergie forty (Then and now)** was the best reel at 1,410 IG views and 4 saves.
- **TikTok** views sit steadily at 650-760 (top of the baseline), ~99% from For You.

## What didn't
- **Pick your side** was the weakest series: 4 reels, 139-374 IG views. The single Then and now, Head to head and Fans say reels all beat every Pick your side reel, but with one reel each that is **not enough yet** to move a weekday.
- **Instagram views at 19:00 IST trail the other desks badly.** Our 7 reels: median 374. Other desks' debate/stat reels at 16:00, 23:30 and 01:30 IST: median ~1,500, some with similar watch times (Xabi 7.0 s, 1,225 views). TikTok shows no such gap, so timing on Instagram is the likeliest cause.
- **TikTok full watch** is ~0.03%, well under the 0.1-0.5% baseline; people leave after 3-6 s of a ~28 s reel. Almost no shares anywhere.
- **X video** is below baseline (8-26 impressions on 3 of 4 days).
- Voice: Reijnders went out on bm_fable (lowest views, 139). One reel: not enough yet.
