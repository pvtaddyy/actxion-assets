## Do less of

- Carousels at 07:30 and 12:00 IST. They reach 25 to 60 people.
- If carousels are still under the median next Sunday, the rule says drop the format, so the two tests below matter.

## Two experiments for the automated carousels
1. **Posting time.** Move every carousel into 18:00 to 21:30 IST (our followers' best hours), at least 45 minutes from a team reel. Compare views with this week's 70.
2. **Cover and story type.** Make the cover a big face plus a 6-word question (like the debate reels that work, e.g. "Big game player or flat track bully?") instead of a news headline, and pick stories about one star player rather than match previews (Forest v City got 6 views, Messi's 10 got 116). Also reuse the debate reel's question on the carousel the same evening.

## The 3 human jobs that matter most this week
1. **App cross-promotion.** The 600K post shows app fans respond (1,143 views). Put an in-app banner and a match-night push pointing to @actxionapp. This is the only lever big enough for the 16 Nov target.
2. **Story polls every day.** Follower reach is only 3% of reach. A daily A or B poll sticker on the combined XI and debate topics brings followers back and tells Instagram they care.
3. **Reply to comments within the first hour** on the combined XI and debate reels, and agree one collab combined XI reel with a fan page of 2,000 to 8,000 followers.
