Account @actxionapp. This week = Mon 9 to Sun 15 Nov, last week = Mon 2 to Sun 8 Nov (IST). Source: Metricool.
Note: Metricool has follower numbers only up to Fri 13 Nov, and Saturday's figures are still filling in. Saturday's posts are only a day old, so they look worse than they will end up.

## The headline
The reels are working and the carousels are not. The three combined XI reels and two stat reels did 1,600 to 2,150 views each. The 17 automated carousels did a median of 50 views, half of last week's level, and none of them got a single share. Followers are still slipping: 4,098 on Friday, down 11 from 4,109 a week earlier.

What this means in plain terms:
- Almost everyone who sees us is a stranger (96%). Our own followers barely see anything: about 70 of them a day. Many of the 4,100 followers came from the old ads and are not active.
- 13,000+ views turned into only 5 new followers. That is a profile problem, not a views problem: people watch a reel and leave without following. The bio and pinned posts need to give them a reason to follow.
- Fewer reels went out this week (14 against 25), which explains most of the drop in views.

## Next week: do more of
1. Combined XI reels: aim for one every day (7). Each one is a near-certain 1,500 to 2,000 views.
2. Stat reels at 18:00 to 20:00 with a one-word answer ("YES or NEVER", "one word in the comments"). Aim for 5.
3. Fan voice reels that mention the app (Xabi or the players, Stick or twist). They beat the plain news reels.
4. Pin the best combined XI reel and fix the bio so viewers know why to follow ("Pick your side on every big football argument. Build your XI in the app").

## Next week: do less of

2. Carousels on the same story as a reel (the Carrick sacking). Let the reel have it.
3. Single images (38 views). Stop until carousels recover.

## Two experiments for the automated carousels
1. **Cover and story type: a stat or ranking cover instead of a news cover.** For 3 of the 7 days, make the 12:05 carousel a "rank it" or "head to head numbers" carousel (for example "Fastest players in the league, ranked") with a big number on the cover, the same idea that makes the stat reels work. Compare its views with the news carousels.
2. **Posting time: move one daily carousel into the 18:00 to 21:30 window.** All carousels currently go out at 07:31, 12:05 and 17:09, before our best hours. Try the 17:09 slot at 21:00 (more than 45 minutes after any reel) for the week and compare.
Measure: median views and shares per carousel against this week's 50 views and 0 shares.

## The 3 human jobs that matter most this week
1. **App cross-promotion (biggest lever).** Put a "Follow us on Instagram" banner in the app and a match-night push before Spain v England and the Premier League return. The 600K installs post showed app users will turn up. This is the only way to close the follower gap.
2. **Daily story polls.** Our followers barely see the feed (about 70 a day). A daily poll sticker ("Kane or Yamal?", "Pochettino: right call?") reminds them we exist and lifts how often Instagram shows them our posts.
3. **Collab one combined XI reel.** Invite a fan page of 2,000 to 8,000 followers to co-post next week's best combined XI with the Collab invite. Then reply to every comment on the combined XI reels in the first hour, because those are where the arguments (and new followers) are.
